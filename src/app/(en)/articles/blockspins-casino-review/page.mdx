## Provably Fair — What It Actually Means

This is the technical claim that separates crypto-first casinos from the rest of the market. The short version:

- Before every spin or bet, the casino generates a cryptographic hash of the game's seed (the random value that determines the outcome)
- That hash is published before you bet — committing the casino to a specific outcome
- After your bet, the casino reveals the seed
- You (or any third-party verifier) can hash the revealed seed and confirm it matches the pre-published hash
- This proves the casino did not change the outcome after seeing your bet

In practice, what this gives you is a verifiable audit trail. You cannot prevent the casino from charging the published house edge (RTP). You CAN prove that individual game outcomes were not manipulated.

Provably fair is most meaningful on Crash and Dice-style games where outcomes are simple integer/float results. On slots, the verification is more technically complex but the principle is the same. For most players, the practical value of provably fair is the **transparency signal** — operators willing to expose their math to verification are operators with cleaner game math overall.
