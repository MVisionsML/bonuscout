## What "Best Willkommensbonus" Actually Means

Before the ranking, the framework. A welcome bonus is the sum of several components:

- **Headline bonus size** — the eye-catching €1,000–€8,000 number
- **Wagering requirement** — typically 30x–50x on the bonus amount
- **Game weighting** — what percentage of each game type counts toward wagering
- **Max bet cap during wagering** — typically €5/spin
- **Bonus expiry** — typically 30 days
- **Free spin component** — separate or bundled, with its own wagering and slot eligibility
- **Cashback/loss-rebate component** — only at some operators

The "best" welcome bonus is the one that maximises expected real-money value across these dimensions for your specific play style. A €8,000 headline at 50x wagering with €5 max bet is mathematically a worse deal for most players than a €2,000 headline at 30x wagering with €10 max bet.
