## How to evaluate the offer when you see it

Whatever the current headline figures are, two structural mechanics carry most of the value determination.

### 1. Wagering scope: bonus-only vs deposit + bonus

A **bonus-only** wagering multiplier applies only to the bonus balance; the deposit is not subject to wagering. A **deposit + bonus** multiplier applies to the sum of both — roughly doubling the required turnover at the same headline multiplier.

Check which scope Talismania publishes on its current promotion page before claiming. The difference matters more than the multiplier itself: a 40x bonus-only requirement is easier to clear than a 30x deposit + bonus requirement on the same-sized welcome.

### 2. Free-spin value

**€0.10 is the industry-standard spin value.** Higher spin values (€0.20 is not unusual at mid-tier operators) proportionally increase the expected real-money value of the free-spin allocation at the same nominal spin count. Verify the current per-spin value in the promotion terms before evaluating the free-spin side of the offer.

### The turnover formula

At any wagering multiplier, the calculation is:

> **Bonus amount × multiplier = required slot turnover before withdrawal.**

Illustrative example (not a Talismania-specific claim): a €100 bonus at a 35x multiplier requires €3,500 in slot wagering. At 40x it would be €4,000. The linear scaling matters over multiple bonus claims — the €500 turnover gap at €100 translates to roughly €20 in expected loss at 96% RTP slots.

Whatever the current Talismania multiplier is when you see the offer, run the formula against the current bonus size before claiming. If the number is not sustainable given your bankroll and expected play time, the bonus is not worth claiming — regardless of its headline size.

## Bonus terms fine print

The mechanics below are the ones most often overlooked when reading a bonus offer. All are per current terms — the categories are the checklist.

- **Max cashout from bonus** — some operators cap the maximum withdrawal derived from bonus winnings.
- **Bonus expiry window** — the deadline for completing wagering before the bonus balance is forfeited. 7, 14, and 30 days are common defaults.
- **Sticky vs non-sticky bonus** — a sticky bonus is deducted from any withdrawal request; a non-sticky bonus is not.
- **Game weighting** — slots typically contribute 100% to wagering, live dealer and table games contribute much less (often 10% and 5% respectively). If you play anything other than slots, the effective wagering requirement is materially higher.
- **Max bet during wagering** — a per-spin cap that applies while the bonus is active. Exceeding it can void the bonus.
- **Free-spin winnings treatment** — whether free-spin winnings drop into a real-money balance or a bonus balance carrying wagering. This is the most common trap in free-spin offers.
